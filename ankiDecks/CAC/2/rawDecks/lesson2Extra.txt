要...了 (yào ... le) Something is about to happen.
就要...了 (jiù yào ... le) Emphasizing immediacy.
快...了 (kuài ... le) Something is happening very soon.
就快...了 (jiù kuài ... le) Very, very soon.
才 (cái) later than expected; not until; used to express a necessary condition.
你比一般的中国人努力吗？ (nǐ bǐ yì bān de zhōng guó rén nǔ lì ma) Are you more hardworking than the average Chinese person?
懒 (lǎn) lazy.
赚钱 (zhuàn qián) to make money; to earn money.
改变 (gǎi biàn) to change; to alter.
我一接到你的电话，就出发。 (wǒ yì jiē dào nǐ de diàn huà, jiù chū fā) As soon as I received your call, I left.
生活 (shēng huó) life; living.
表弟 (biǎo dì) younger male cousin.
他们在你的家住多长时间？ (tā men zài nǐ de jiā zhù duō cháng shí jiān) How long have they stayed at your house?
他20岁就结婚了。 (tā èr shí suì jiù jié hūn le) He got married at 20.
他60岁才结婚。 (tā liù shí suì cái jié hūn) He didn’t get married until he was 60.
醉了 (zuì le) drunk; intoxicated.
他喝了七八杯酒，才醉。 (tā hē le qī bā bēi jiǔ, cái zuì) He drank seven or eight glasses of wine before getting drunk.
我叫了杨安薄两次，她才回答。 (wǒ jiào le yáng ān bó liǎng cì, tā cái huí dá) I called Yang Anbo twice before she answered.
回答 (huí dá) to answer; reply.
我叫了微洛一次，她就回答了。 (wǒ jiào le wēi luò yī cì, tā jiù huí dá le) I called Weiluo once and she answered right away.
上了高铁以后，他才发现，他坐了错的车。 (shàng le gāo tiě yǐ hòu, tā cái fā xiàn, tā zuò le cuò de chē) After getting on the high-speed train, he only then realized he had boarded the wrong train.
很快地告诉我们 (hěn kuài de gào sù wǒ men) Tell us very quickly.
