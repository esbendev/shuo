* 几 (jǐ) - is used when the number is expected to be small (usually under 10).
* 多 (duō) - when a number is slightly over a whole number, it must be a full unit (like years,cups,hours,etc)
* 几十 (jǐshí) - several tens
* 几百 (jǐbǎi) - several hundreds
* 多/几 (duō/jǐ) - used afer round numbers (10,20,30,...) to mean “more than” or “over”
* 已经...了 (yǐjīng…le) - to emphazise that something has already happened.
