1. 西瓜 (xīguā - watermelon - n.)
◦ 这个 西瓜 又 大 又 甜。(zhè ge xīguā yòu dà yòu tián)
This watermelon also big also sweet.
(This watermelon is big and sweet.)
◦ 你 想 吃 西瓜 吗?(nǐ xiǎng chī xīguā ma?)
You want eat watermelon yes or no?
(Do you want to eat watermelon?)

2. 蛋糕 (dàngāo - cake - n.)
◦ 别 吃 太多 蛋糕 了!(bié chī tàiduō dàngāo le!)
Don't eat too much cake!
(Don't eat too much cake!)
◦ 我 给 你 买 了 一个 生日 蛋糕。(wǒ gěi nǐ mǎi le yí ge shēngrì dàngāo)
I for you buy already one birthday cake.
(I bought you a birthday cake.)

3. 票 (piào - ticket - n.)
◦ 我 在 网上 买 电影 票 吧。(wǒ zài wǎngshàng mǎi diànyǐng piào ba.)
I at online buy movie ticket suggestion mark.
(Let me buy movie tickets online./ I’ll just buy movie tickets online.)
◦ 请问 我 要 去 哪里 买 车票?(qǐngwèn wǒ yào qù nǎli mǎi
chēpiào?)
Excuse me, I need go where buy bus ticket?
(Excuse me, where can I buy a bus ticket?)

4. 阿姨 (āyí - aunt - n.)
◦ 她的 阿姨 是 工程师。(tā de āyí shì gōngchéngshī.)
Her aunt is engineer.
(Her aunt is an engineer.)
◦ 你 阿姨 叫 你。(nǐ āyí jiào nǐ.)
Your aunt call you.
(Your aunt is asking for you.)
5. 同事 (tóngshì - colleague - n.)
◦ 我的 同事 不 都 是 中国人。(wǒ de tóngshì bù dōu shì zhōngguó rén.)
My colleague not all are Chinese. (Not all my colleagues are Chinese.)
◦ 我 不 太 喜欢 和 同事 聊天。(wǒ bú tài xǐhuan hé tóngshì liáotiān.)
I not very like with colleague chat.
(I don’t like chatting with colleagues much.)

6. 同学 (tóngxué - classmate - n.)

◦ 我们 班 有 两个 新 同学。(wǒmen bān yǒu liǎng ge xīn tóngxué.)
Our class have two new classmates.
(We have two new classmates in our class.)
◦ 我们 班 只 有 一个 女 同学。(wǒmen bān zhǐ yǒu yí ge nǚ tóngxué.)
Our class only have one female classmate.
(We only have one female classmate in our class.)

7. 结婚 (jiéhūn - to marry - v.)
◦ 他 跟 一个 律师 结婚了。(tā gēn yí ge lǜshī jiéhūn le.)
He with one lawyer married.
(He married a lawyer.)
◦ 小王 去年 刚 结婚。(xiǎo wáng qùnián gāng jiéhūn.)
Little Wang last year just married.
(Xiao Wang just got married last year.)

8. 报名 (bàomíng - to sign up - v.)
◦ 我 报名了 老师的 课。(wǒ bàomíng le lǎoshī de kè.)
I signed up teacher's class.
(I signed up for the teacher's class.)
◦ 我们 一起 去 报名 吧。(wǒmen yīqǐ qù bàomíng ba.)
We together go sign up suggestion mark.
(Let’s go sign up together.)
9. 搬家 (bānjiā - to move - v.)
◦ 我 不 常 搬家。(wǒ bù cháng bānjiā.)
I not often move.
(I don’t move often.)
◦ 他 从 北京 搬 到 了 上海。(tā cóng běijīng bāndào le
shànghǎi.)
He from Beijing moved to already Shanghai.
(He moved from Beijing to Shanghai.)

10. 开始 (kāishǐ - to begin - v. n.)

◦ 电影 什么时候 开始?(diànyǐng shénme shíhòu kāishǐ?)
Movie when start?
(When does the movie start?)
◦ 我 每天 早上 八点 开始 工作。(wǒ měitiān zǎoshàng bā diǎn
kāishǐ gōngzuò.)
I every day morning eight o'clock begin work.
(I start working at 8 AM every day.)

11. 北方/南方 (běifāng/nánfāng - northern/southern part - n.)
◦ 我 家 在 中国 北方。(wǒ jiā zài zhōngguó běifāng.)
My home is in China northern part.
(My home is in northern China.)
◦ 北方人 说 "一点儿",南方人 说 "一点"。(běifāng rén
shuō "yīdiǎn er", nánfāng rén shuō "yīdiǎn".)
Northern people say "a little(r)”, southern people say "a little".
(Northern people say "a little(r),” southern people say "a little.")

12. 东部/西部 (dōngbù/xībù - eastern/western part - n.)
◦ 我 没有 去过 中国 西部。(wǒ méiyou qùguo zhōngguó xībù.)
I have not been to China western part.
(I have not been to western China.)
◦ 现在 是 美国 东部 时间 下午 五点。(xiànzài shì měiguó
dōngbù shíjiān xiàwǔ wǔ diǎn.)
Now is America eastern part time afternoon five o'clock.
(Now it’s 5 PM Eastern Time in the USA.)
13. 遍 (biàn - time; used for repetition - measure word)
◦ 你 可以 再 说 一遍 吗?(nǐ kěyǐ zài shuō yī biàn ma?)
You can again say one time yes or no?
(Can you say it again?)
◦ 这本书 非常 好看, 我 看了 两遍!(zhè běn shū fēicháng
hǎokàn, wǒ kàn le liǎng biàn!)
This book very interesting, I read already two times!
(This book is very interesting; I’ve read it twice!)

14. 讲 (jiǎng - to talk, to explain - v.)
◦ 老师, 请 你 讲一讲 怎么 用 “遍”。(lǎoshī, qǐng nǐ jiǎng yī jiǎng
zěnme yòng "biàn".)
Teacher, please you talk a bit how use "biàn".
(Teacher, please explain how to use “biàn.”)
◦ 我 不 会 讲 上海话。(wǒ bú huì jiǎng shànghǎi huà.)
I not can speak Shanghai dialect.
(I can't speak Shanghainese.)

15. 故事 (gùshi - story - n.)
◦ 这个 孩子 想 再 听 一遍 “西瓜的 故事”。(zhège háizi xiǎng
zài tīng yī biàn "xīguā de gùshi".)
This child wants again hear one time "watermelon's story".
(This child wants to hear the "watermelon story" again.)
◦ 我 给 你 讲 一个故事 吧。(wǒ gěi nǐ jiǎng yí ge gùshi ba.)
I for you tell one story suggestion mark.
(Let me tell you a story.)
